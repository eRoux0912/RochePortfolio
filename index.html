Selected Work

AMR Tool Hire

AMR Tool Hire had no website at all when I approached them — like a lot of small, established businesses, they were running purely on word of mouth. I built their site from scratch: clean semantic markup, compressed images, and on-page SEO, so people could actually find them online for the first time. I still maintain it today, keeping it fast and their business info accurate as things change.

Superior Plant Hire

Superior Plant Hire had over a decade serving St. Helena Bay with no website to show for it. I built theirs from scratch, centered around a wide service catalog: diggers, skips, sand, stone, and more. I regrouped everything into clear categories, optimized every image and video, and added structured data so Google surfaces their real hours and location. The result is a fast, fully live site that finally puts them online.
